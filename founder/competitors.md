# Competitors: AI trip planning on mobile

Researched 2026-11-20 from public search results. Limits, stated once: I read search summaries and third-party reviews, not each vendor's own price page or the app stores directly, so prices are "as reported" and need confirming. Store ratings for most products were not found. No Thai-market usage data was found.

## 2. Price range for the comparable item (a consumer plan that unlocks full planning)

- Lowest: free (Mindtrip, Wonderplan, the booking-app assistants, general chatbots).
- Paid tiers found: about $39.99/year (Wanderlog Pro) and about $49/year (Layla Premium).
- AiGo today: $4.99/month for 50 credits, about $59.88/year, and one generated trip costs 10 credits or more. AiGo is the only one in this set that meters by credit, and its yearly cost is above both paid competitors.

## 4. What customers complain about

Evidence is thin for every theme (under three reviews read each, plus two commissioned studies). Treat as leads, not findings.

1. **AI plans contain errors.** A 2024 test by SEO Travel found 90% of ChatGPT two-day itineraries had at least one error, 52% sent people to places outside opening hours and one in four suggested a closed place ([Travolution](https://travolution.com/news/marketing/survey-finds-chatgpt-itineraries-to-be-riddled-with-inaccuracies)). A 2026 Greetwell survey of US adults reported 1 in 6 AI-using travellers were recommended something that was not real ([Stacker](https://stacker.com/stories/travel/survey-ai-hallucinated-travel-destinations-1-6-users)). Both were commissioned by companies with an interest.
2. **Subscription billing and cancelling.** Wanderlog on Trustpilot: "I don't know how this app charged my bank account without me subscribing" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com)). Layla on Trustpilot: "there is no option how to unsubscribe" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/layla.ai)).
3. **Yearly-only plans.** Wanderlog App Store review: "I wish there was a monthly subscription plan instead of yearly" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog/id1476732439)). People plan a few trips a year.
4. **Offline is paywalled.** Wanderlog offline access is reported as a Pro feature at about $40/year ([Pilot comparison](https://www.pilotplans.com/compare/wanderlog-alternative-feature-comparison), a competitor's page). One Trustpilot reviewer says it worked offline without paying, so this is contested.
5. **Bugs and sync on mobile.** Wanderlog Trustpilot: "Synchronisation issues, tons of bugs especially working with it on mobile" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com)).

## 5. The gap

No clear gap was found for "an AI that writes an itinerary". That job is now free inside Google Maps, Agoda, Trip.com and every general chatbot.

Three narrower openings, each needing proof:

- **A plan you can trust.** The best-documented complaint is wrong hours and closed or invented places. AiGo's backend already has venue and opening-hours checks and weather replanning. Nothing found shows a competitor leading with verification. Unproven that AiGo's checks are better; this needs a side-by-side test.
- **Pay for a trip, not a year.** Complaints about yearly plans and cancelling suggest a one-off per-trip price could be an advantage. AiGo's credits are closer to this than a subscription, but the current ladder costs more per year than both paid rivals.
- **Thai-first, going abroad.** No stand-alone AI planner was found that is built in Thai for Thais travelling abroad; the TAT app is domestic only. But Trip.com's assistant supports Thai and general chatbots speak Thai, so language alone is not a gap. The gap would have to be Thai-specific content: visa rules for Thai passports, Thai payment, budgets in baht. Not verified.

## 6. The threat

- **Google Maps.** AI itineraries now sit inside the map travellers already navigate with, in 200+ countries, at no stated price. It needs nothing new to copy "plan and carry on a map".
- **Agoda and Trip.com.** Both plan and book in one conversation, already hold the traveller's payment details, and earn from the booking, so they can give planning away. Agoda is widely used by Thai travellers (not measured here).
- AiGo depends on affiliate links to these same companies for its second income line.
